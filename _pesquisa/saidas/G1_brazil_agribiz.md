## 1. Pathways to Rural Sustainability: Opportunities and Challenges in the Creation of an Agrotechnological District in Ingaí City, Brazil
- Autores: Caroline Mendonça Nogueira Paiva; Derick Quintino; Thacyo Bruno Custódio de Morais; Elisa Guimarães Cozadi et al.
- Ano: 2024 | Periodico: Agriculture | Tipo: article
- DOI: 10.3390/agriculture14122185 | Citacoes: 3 | OA: True
- Areas: Innovation and Socioeconomic Development, Rural Development and Agriculture
- Link: https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0472/14/12/2185/pdf?version=1733123493
- Resumo: An agrotechnological district (ATD) integrates sustainable agricultural practices and technologies, aiming to transform rural communities by stimulating socioeconomic development and addressing the UN Sustainable Development Goals. Ingaí, a dairy-producing municipality in Minas Gerais, Brazil, stands to benefit from the implementation of an ATD. This study aimed to identify the opportunities and challenges for the implementation of an ATD in the municipality, considering its socioeconomic characteristics, the level of producers’ technological adoption, connectivity infrastructure, support networks, and rural and market management.
